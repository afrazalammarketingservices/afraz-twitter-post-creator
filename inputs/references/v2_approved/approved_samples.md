## Mon, seo_fix, text
Your sitemap is a hint, not an order.

Google's own docs call a sitemap "merely a hint". Submitting one does not force Google to index your new products.

What helps more: link each new product from a live collection page, then run it through URL Inspection in Search Console.

## Thu, myth_or_mistake, text
On Shopify, one product can live at two URLs:

/collections/shoes/products/red-sneaker
/products/red-sneaker

Shopify's canonical tag points Google to the short one, so it's usually fine.

The waste starts when themes link the long one everywhere. Which does your menu use?
