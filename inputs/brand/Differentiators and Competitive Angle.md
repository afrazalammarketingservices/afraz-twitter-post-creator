# Differentiators and Competitive Angle

## The core wedge: consultant, not agency
Every page returns to one argument - you get senior-level work directly from the person doing it, without the agency layer.

> The efficiency of a specialist without the overhead of a bloated agency.

## Four unique angles worth protecting
1. **SEO included free in every website build.** Most developers charge extra to make a site SEO-friendly. AAMS bakes titles, meta, schema, structure, speed and analytics into every build at no extra cost. This is the strongest single offer differentiator in the portfolio.
2. **AI as the efficiency layer, not a separate product.** AI is stated to accelerate SEO, ads, web and reporting across the board, while strategy stays human. "AI does the heavy lifting; I make the decisions."
3. **Radical honesty about stage.** Openly labels itself early-stage, labels own-brand numbers as own-brand, and distinguishes account-level totals from single-campaign results. Rare and highly defensible.
4. **Full-stack under one person.** Web + SEO + Google Ads + Meta Ads + AI + design. No hand-offs between vendors, one integrated strategy, one report.
