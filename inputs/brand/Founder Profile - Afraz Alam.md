## The story (positioning hook)
> I learned marketing by doing the actual work.

For over 5 years he has built, run and marketed his own websites across many niches - including the affiliate brand **USA Tech Deals**, the marketing blog **Techfee**, and real campaigns like the **BusiQueens** event. Not theory from a course. Hands-on, with real budgets and real data.

**This is the single strongest narrative asset in the brand.** It answers "why you and not an agency" without needing a client roster.
