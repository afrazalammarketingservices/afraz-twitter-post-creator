# Voice and Messaging Bank

## Tone rules derived from the site
1. **Plain language, no jargon.** Explicitly promised: "no confusing jargon."
2. **Short, direct sentences.** Claims are stated then immediately qualified with honesty.
3. **Second person.** Almost everything is "you" and "your business", not "our clients".
4. **First person singular.** Always "I", never "we". This is deliberate and reinforces the solo-consultant positioning. **Never write "we" in AAMS copy.**
5. **Numbers over adjectives.** Real figures (Rs 1.4 CPC, 85,251 reached) do the persuading.
6. **An honest qualifier follows every strong claim.** "No ethical marketer can guarantee exact results."
7. **Anti-vanity-metric framing.** Leads, calls, sales, revenue, ROI. Never likes, reach or impressions as the goal.

## Signature phrases (reuse verbatim)
- "leads, calls, and revenue, not vanity metrics"
- "Strategy-first approach, ROI-focused execution, direct transparent communication"
- "You work directly with me, not an account manager"
- "the efficiency of a specialist without the overhead of a bloated agency"
- "senior-level work without agency overhead, scaled to your budget"
- "Honest numbers, not inflated claims"
- "no guesswork" / "no shortcuts" / "no fluff"
- "built to rank, convert, and grow"
- "AI does the heavy lifting; I make the decisions"
- "real data from real campaigns"
- "Clear answers before you get started"
- "the person actually doing the work"

## Headline formulas that repeat across the site
**Pattern A:** [Service] services that [outcome] and [outcome].
- "SEO services that get you found and turn searches into leads."
- "Google Ads management services that turn searches into leads and sales."
- "Meta Ads management services that turn Facebook and Instagram into leads and sales."
- "Graphic design services that make your brand look sharp and convert."

**Pattern B:** Why most [channel] [fails] - the problem-agitation block
**Pattern C:** Your [channel], managed end to end
**Pattern D:** A [channel] account built to perform
**Pattern E:** [Service] questions, answered honestly

## The 3-problem agitation pattern
Every service page uses exactly three problems before presenting the solution:

| Service | Problem 1 | Problem 2 | Problem 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEO | Buried below competitors | Paying for every click | Great site, no traffic |
| Google Ads | Paying for the wrong clicks | No conversion tracking | Weak ads and landing pages |
| Meta Ads | Boosting, not targeting | Weak, skippable creative | No pixel or tracking |
| Web Dev | Slow and not found on Google | Looks good but does not convert | Hard to manage and scale |
| AI Marketing | Content is slow and expensive | Time lost to manual work | Invisible in AI search |
| All-in-One | Disconnected channels | Too many vendors to manage | No single owner of results |

> [!tip] This is a reusable content engine
> Each cell is a carousel slide, an ad hook, or a blog intro. 18 ready-made angles.

## Service badge taxonomy (keep consistent)
- FLAGSHIP - Website Development
- VISIBILITY - SEO
- ACQUISITION - Google Ads, Meta Ads
- AUTOMATION - AI Marketing
- GROWTH - All-in-One Digital Marketing
- CREATIVE - Graphic Design

## Structural pattern of every service page
1. Badge and category label
2. H1 with outcome promise
3. Sub-line with mechanism
4. Two CTAs (book a call + jump link)
5. Trust strip (3 short proof phrases)
6. "Every campaign includes" checklist box
7. WHAT YOU GET - keyword-rich SEO paragraph in bold
8. Problem agitation (3 problems)
9. WHAT I MANAGE - 8 numbered capability cards
10. Two engagement tiers or campaign types
11. WHO IT IS FOR - 7 industries + a fallback card
12. WHY WORK WITH ME - 6 reasons
13. HOW IT WORKS - 4-step process
14. WHAT YOU GET - 8 deliverables
15. Free plan lead magnet
16. FAQ (6-7 questions)
17. Closing CTA

**Reuse this skeleton for any new service page.**

## Words to avoid
guaranteed rankings, overnight results, viral, cheap, number 1 on Google, we/our team, industry-leading, cutting-edge
