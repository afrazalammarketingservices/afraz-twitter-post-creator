# ICP and Target Industries

## The 7 core industries (repeated on every service page)

|---|---|
| **Local Businesses** | Dominate local market, more foot traffic and inquiries |
| **Clinics and Healthcare** | More patient appointments for doctors and clinics |
| **Gyms and Fitness** | More memberships and trial sign-ups |
| **Real Estate** | High-quality property leads for agents and firms |
| **Education and Coaching** | Increase student admissions for coaching centres |
| **Service Businesses** | Leads for lawyers, CAs and professionals |
| **E-commerce and D2C** | Scale online sales for direct-to-consumer brands |

Additional segments named on specific pages:
- **Events and Local Promotions** (Meta Ads) - proven with BusiQueens
- **Coaches and Creators** (AI Marketing, Graphic Design)
- **Busy Founders** (AI Marketing, All-in-One)
- **Startups** (Graphic Design)
- **Retail and Local Shops** (Kolkata page)

## Best-fit clients (site-stated)
- Businesses serious about growth
- Founders who value strategy over shortcuts
- Brands willing to invest in marketing systems
- Businesses focused on ROI and long-term results

## Explicitly NOT a fit (site-stated)
- Looking for overnight results
- Only want the cheapest option
- No clarity or commitment
- Focused only on vanity metrics

> [!tip] The disqualification block is a strong sales asset
> Stating who you turn away raises perceived selectivity and filters bad leads before the call. Keep it on every major page.
