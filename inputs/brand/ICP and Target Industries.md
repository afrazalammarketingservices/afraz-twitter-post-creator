# ICP and Target Industries

> Changed 2026-10-31 by Afraz: Shopify, D2C and ecommerce brands are the only target market. The personas below are the only ones to write for. Everything that was here before is kept, text unchanged, under "Retired personas, do not use".

## Positioning
Afraz Alam, Digital Marketing Consultant for Shopify, D2C and ecommerce brands, USA first. Services: conversion tracking (GA4, GTM, Meta Pixel, Conversions API), high-converting landing and product pages, Meta and Google ads (including Google Shopping and Merchant Center), ecommerce SEO and AI search visibility.

## Persona 1: Shopify founder running Meta ads
- Founder or owner of a Shopify / D2C / ecommerce brand doing roughly $10k to $200k a month in sales.
- Already running Meta ads (often Google ads too), small team or owner-led marketing.
- Their words: ROAS, break-even ROAS, CAC, MER, AOV, conversion rate, add to cart, checkout, product page, creative, Advantage+, Pixel, CAPI, Shopify, retargeting, email capture.

## Persona 2: Head of growth at a D2C brand
- Head of marketing or growth at a Shopify, D2C or ecommerce brand in the same $10k to $200k a month range, already running Meta or Google ads.
- Owns the numbers: revenue that does not match between Shopify, GA4 and Meta, budget split, Merchant Center and Shopping feeds, GA4 ecommerce events.
- Also covers the ecommerce ads, CRO and tracking practitioners these founders follow on X.

## Geography
- US first in examples, platforms and spelling.
- Secondary: UK, Canada, Australia, UAE.

## Not readers (never write for them)
Home-service owners (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing), local service businesses, coaches, consultants, Amazon-only sellers, dropshipping stores.

### Best-fit clients (site-stated)
- Businesses serious about growth
- Founders who value strategy over shortcuts
- Brands willing to invest in marketing systems
- Businesses focused on ROI and long-term results

### Explicitly NOT a fit (site-stated)
- Looking for overnight results
- Only want the cheapest option
- No clarity or commitment
- Focused only on vanity metrics

> [!tip] The disqualification block is a strong sales asset
> Stating who you turn away raises perceived selectivity and filters bad leads before the call. Keep it on every major page.
